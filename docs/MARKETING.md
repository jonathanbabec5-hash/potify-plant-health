# Plant Health Journal marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Grow journal keepers. **Offer.** Help a grower see how conditions and care change over a plant's lifetime.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show two dated observations from a fictional plant and how the journal preserves them on reload. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Record a first observation. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 8 completed sample entries. |

### Sales test

Pair the free timeline with a proposed $8 printable journal. Test three requests for a sample before paying for printed inventory. Offer a printable season journal after validating demand with three separately opted-in readers.

### Measurement and decision

Use `plant-health-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Personal device histories and forecasts require their own verification. This export is application source. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
